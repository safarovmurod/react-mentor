## Gaps found (matching the master prompt's complaints)

1. **25 two-day introductory modules** — python, backend, mobile, java, csharp, go, rust, php, ruby, sql, bash, nodejs, mongodb, swift, kotlin, ux-design, digital-marketing, data-analytics, business-foundations, communication, content-creation, english-foundations, digital-literacy, personal-productivity, management-foundations. Each is honestly labeled “introductory”, but the master prompt requires beginner tracks to become real 14–30+ day curricula.
2. **Premium modules are only 3 days each** — javascript-pro, backend-pro, ux-pro, business-pro (Pro) and architecture-expert (Expert). Target: 30–60 days of deep content per paid course.
3. **No AI education courses at all** — AI Fundamentals, Prompt Engineering, Context Engineering, Agent Harness, AI Agents, RAG Fundamentals do not exist. The master prompt provides exact 14-day syllabi for three of them (§7 AI Fundamentals, §8 Context Engineering, §9 Agent Harness).
4. **No Algorithms courses** — neither the free Beginner track nor the 42-day Advanced Algorithms Pro track.
5. **No TypeScript course** in the catalog (TypeScript appears only inside React/Next.js courses).
6. Schema constraint: `src/lib/courses/schema.ts` limits `day` to ≤ 30 — sufficient for 14-day AI courses, must be revisited for 30–60+ day expansions.
7. Taxonomy hard-codes 10 categories / 6 programming subtracks (`tests/course-taxonomy.test.ts`); adding the AI Engineering and Algorithms categories requires updating that contract plus `CATEGORY_ICONS` in the catalog UI.
8. CSS course has 5 unscheduled library lessons only (`publishedDays: 5` comes from the fallback plan path).

## Non-gaps (things the master prompt worried about that are already correct)

- Free beginner content stays free; premium bodies/solutions are never sent to unauthorized clients (`tests/premium-*.test.ts`).
- No fake payments/reviews/certificates/AI answers anywhere; unsupported runtimes state their limits (e.g. “server does not execute Python code”).
- `publishedDays` reflects reality — no planned days are shown as published.
- Progress survives refresh; account switching isolates data (tested).
